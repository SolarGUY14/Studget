# Product Specification

## Product direction

Studget is a college-first personal-finance platform designed to become useful to anyone who wants clearer, more actionable guidance about their money. Its central product question is:

> Given my available money, spending habits, obligations, and goals, how much can I safely spend right now?

The product should be useful for personal daily use and eventually broader adoption, with an application on iOS and Android and a responsive website on laptops. Users should be able to quickly understand their financial position, spending trends, and possible next actions.

The product direction includes:

- Connected checking, savings, and credit accounts
- Automated transaction ingestion and categorization
- Daily, weekly, and monthly budgets
- Semester and custom-period budgets
- Category budgets
- Safe-to-spend calculations
- Financial runway projections
- Savings goals
- Investing-readiness goals
- Personalized spending insights
- An eventual AI financial copilot grounded in the user's financial data
- Future budget and obligation notifications with user-controlled preferences
- Future read-only brokerage and portfolio visibility, investigating Fidelity first; provider coverage and access must be verified before implementation

## MVP exclusions

The MVP does not include:

- Money movement
- Securities trading
- Cryptocurrency trading
- Tax filing
- Loan origination
- Specific-stock recommendations
